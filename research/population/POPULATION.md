# Workload population (programme 2, WP8; exploratory)

Generated by `research/population/population.py` from `data/windows_600s.csv.gz`. 240 workloads x 8 tenants, split once into tuning and evaluation halves (seed 20261112). Tenants are a construction: the traces carry no tenant identity. Rates are rescaled to a target drawn log-uniformly in (0.5, 20.0) rps.

Tenants per stream: azure2024/code 342, azure2024/conv 334, burstgpt/ChatGPT/api 325, burstgpt/ChatGPT/conv 312, burstgpt/GPT-4/api 299, burstgpt/GPT-4/conv 308.

Rescaling a window's rate keeps the modulation's relative shape but not its weight against Poisson noise: the modulation variance scales with the square of the factor, the Poisson part with the factor, so a busy stream (Azure, tens of requests per second) rescaled to a few requests per second keeps little of its autocorrelation. The range check passes; the medians show the attenuation. Native replay (WP8 step 5: superposing real days at their own timestamps) is the construction that keeps it.
