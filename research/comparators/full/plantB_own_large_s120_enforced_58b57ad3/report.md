## Arms and parameter spaces

**joint**: the joint planner: one tenant's three knobs searched together on the move lattice, tenants coordinated by convergent sweeps on one shared objective. Maps to: controller.JCACController, knob_search 'joint' (registered arm jcac_converged), via tunable_planner.TunablePlanner.

| parameter | range | default |
|---|---|---|
| `switch_penalty` | log-uniform [0.001, 0.2] | 0.05 |
| `horizon_steps` | integer 1..6 | 6 |
| `forecast_method` | {persistence, trend, holt, seasonal} | trend |
| `weight_beta` | log-uniform [0.25, 32] | 2.0 |
| `weight_gamma` | uniform [0, 2] | 0.5 |

**per_knob**: the per-knob planner: each knob best-responded alone on the shared objective, winners combined. Maps to: knob_search 'decoupled' (registered arm jcac_decoupled).

| parameter | range | default |
|---|---|---|

**one_knob**: the iterated one-knob planner: one knob at a time to a fixed point, never a two-knob move. Maps to: knob_search 'knobwise' (registered arm jcac_knobwise).

## Caveats

- Exploratory: nothing here is registered; the class rule, the spaces and the 64-configuration budget are fixed in `research/comparators/` before any result.
- Paired comparisons share demand: every arm and configuration of a workload saw the same seeded demand (harness shared seeds).
- LB1, LB2 and UB assume the planners' move limits (+/-2 replicas, one cache level per interval). The default-behaviour HPA, the cluster-cap trim and the budget gate may move further, and the planners' infeasible-lattice fallback jumps to the floor; only LB0 bounds every arm. `clamp viol.` is the share of tenant-steps that left the limits.
