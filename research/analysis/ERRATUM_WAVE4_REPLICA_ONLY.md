# Erratum: the replica-only arm of B1 and B1′ ran misconfigured

Generated by `erratum_wave4_replica_only.py` from the committed per-run exports under `eval/results/wave4_calibrated_plane_evidence/runs/` and the committed run tables. Descriptive: no hypothesis, no verdict. The verdict records stand as committed; this record says which of their readings measured a misconfigured arm.

## Mechanism

The WL-H2 knob preflight probes tenant `t00`, and its last write pins `t00` to the largest tier with the cache at 0 MB. Operator arms (`jcac`, `jcac-calibrated`, `cache-only`, `tier-only`) have their Policy knobs re-pushed when the reconciler resumes. The non-operator `replica-only` arm had its default knobs pushed before the probe and never again, so `t00` served the scored window on the largest tier with no cache. Found by the audit of 2026-09-26 and fixed in `eval/harness/cluster_backend.py` (`prepare_live_ai_knobs` pushes the defaults after the probe and reads them back).

## Finding

**CONTAMINATED.** In every AI-cell run `replica-only` sent 1246–2988 requests to the largest tier; every other arm sent 12, which is the preflight probe's own traffic. Its cache hit rate is one tenant's worth below the others'. `replica-only`'s costs in these cells are not a measurement of a replica-only controller.
