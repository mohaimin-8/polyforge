## Reading

The tier-share table is the structural answer: where the treatment's cost
win is dominated by infrastructure spend (replicas + cache memory), no
re-pricing of the tier axis can undo it; where tier spend is material, the
sweep shows how far the ratios must move before the sign changes. Flip
points, if any, are listed verbatim — including them is the point. All of
this holds decisions fixed; the pre-registered rerun measures what the
controllers do when they can *react* to the new economy.
