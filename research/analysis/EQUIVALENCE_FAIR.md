## Reading

- A comparison is a tie in the strong sense only where its margin ruled out is small next to the comparator's J and its MDE; a large margin means the data are consistent with a real difference either way.
- The per-metric rows show what J nets out: a cost difference in one direction and a lateness difference in the other can sum to a ΔJ near zero.
- Non-dominance counts several arms per design point; a count near the number of design points means the arm is on the front almost everywhere, not that it wins.
