## Soundness of the comparison

The two sides are deliberately asymmetric, and the asymmetry is what makes a positive gap a proof rather than an artefact:

- the **reactive** side is a *lower bound* — the reach clamp and the budget filter are both relaxed, which can only shrink the minimum, so any real reactive controller pays at least this;
- the **predictive** side is an *achievable trajectory* — reach, budget and knob bounds are all enforced and the trajectory closes into a cycle, so a controller really can realise it.

Comparing two lower bounds would prove nothing.

## MECHANISM — the replacement test (runnable)

The row above exists to show the separation is caused by observational aliasing rather than by arithmetic. That claim is worth keeping, so it is re-derived in a form that runs. Removing *only* the ability to tell orbit positions apart — capacity requirements untouched — must collapse or invert the gap:


| orbit | aliased gap | de-aliased gap | effect of removing aliasing |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `flash` | +49.0% | -19.5% | **gap inverts** — the entire separation was the information asymmetry |
| `ramp_gentle` | +2.7% | +0.0% | **gap vanishes exactly** |

Both directions land as the theorem predicts, and both are computed above rather than asserted.

A second, independent form of the same test uses orbits that are aliasing-free **by construction** rather than by perturbation — a monotone sweep of the identical 0.5×–6.0× envelope, where every position carries a distinct demand and no two are near-ties:

Every one is negative: with nothing to be uncertain about, the relaxed reactive floor sits *below* an achievable predictive cycle, which must respect the reach clamp. Aliasing is the whole mechanism.


## Scope — what this record does NOT claim

- This is a **single-tenant** derivation. Cluster caps couple tenants and the coupling is load-bearing, not an optional refinement: ignoring caps flips `flash_crud`'s sign, and an equal per-tenant share makes that cell infeasible. The multi-tenant extension is WP13 proper; until it lands, the correspondence with the campaigns is **suggestive structural corroboration at one operating point**, not a validated correspondence, and must be cited that way.
- The derived reactive class is an *idealised* policy keyed on the exact demand. Real HPA/KEDA additionally **lag**, which this theorem does not model, so measured deltas contain reactive lag on top of the separation derived here.
- The floor is a statement about the **plant and its actuation authority**, not about JCAC's tuning: the deployed controller minimises a composite objective and may knowingly spend violation to save money.
