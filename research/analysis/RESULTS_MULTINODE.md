## What this does and does not license

Every node here is a container on one machine sharing one kernel, one disk and one NIC. A PASS removes **single node** from W7's residue and leaves **single machine** standing — which §8 already lists as permanent. Nothing here kills a node, so nothing here speaks to failure behaviour, partitions or zones.
