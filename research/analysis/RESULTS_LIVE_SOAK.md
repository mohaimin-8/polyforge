It records **7 of 8 injections showing observed perturbation followed by recovery without intervention**, and the eighth (the final throttle) as injected with *no observed perturbation at all* — not padded into an eighth recovery.

**That evidence is reported here as an observation, not as a passed hypothesis.** It was collected by an observer watching a run that turns out to have been invalid, and it is qualitative where the prereg demanded quantitative. Quoting it as "SK-H1 passed, 7/8" would be exactly the laundering this record exists to prevent.

## Root-cause lead (not confirmed)

Two of the sixteen `control-plane` pods show exactly one restart each, timed at roughly one minute after T0. `kubectl port-forward service/…` resolves to **one specific pod** when it starts and does not follow the Service if that pod is replaced; the harness spawns it once (`cluster_backend.execute`, `subprocess.Popen`, output to `DEVNULL`) and never health-checks or restarts it for the life of a run. If the forward pinned to one of those two pods, that is a real mechanism for sustained delivery failure.

**It does not cleanly explain 36.4% on its own** — a single dead pinned pod for 24 h would read closer to 100% than to a third. So this is a lead, not a diagnosis, and it is recorded as one.

**The evidence that would settle it was destroyed by the harness.** `k6-summary.json` — which carries the failure *timeline*, and would show whether the failures began at minute one or accumulated late — was written into a `tempfile.TemporaryDirectory` and deleted when the run exited. Fixing that is step 1 of attempt 3, and it is the reason this record cannot say more.
