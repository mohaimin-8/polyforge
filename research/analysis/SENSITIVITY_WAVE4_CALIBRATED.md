## Why

Ten-second buckets of a controlled system are not independent draws: a plan persists across control steps, so consecutive cost deltas are positively correlated and an i.i.d. bootstrap understates the interval. The moving-block bootstrap (blocks of consecutive buckets drawn with replacement within each rep, reps pooled, 10000 resamples, seed 20260915) preserves within-block dependence; block length 5 (50 s) exceeds the longest lag-1 correlation scale observed and is the decisive one here.
