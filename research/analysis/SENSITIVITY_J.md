## Reading

Across all campaigns and baselines: **416/425** grid cells
preserve the win (paired mean ΔJ < 0 at p < 0.01). Flip cells, if any, are
listed verbatim above — including them is the point of the exercise. A flip
only at extreme weights (e.g. w_v=4 with w_f=0) bounds the claim rather than
refuting it; a flip near the pre-registered cell would be reportable as a
genuine fragility. Either way this section is exploratory context for the
threats-to-validity chapter, not a new result.
