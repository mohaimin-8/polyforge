# Can SK-H1's rule tell a fault from a quiet minute? (W2)

Generated by `analysis_skh1_discriminability.py`. **Descriptive, no pre-registration, and it revises nothing.** `RESULTS_LIVE_SOAK_V8.md` stands exactly as committed and SK-H1 stays **FAILED**; this measures a claim that record makes in prose about *why* it failed.


## The question

V8 reports SK-H1 FAIL on 1 of 8 injections, and observes that `crud_p95` oscillates on a roughly four-minute cycle present before, during and after the failing fault — so the frozen rule, which compares a recovery **maximum** against a pre-window **median**, is scored peak-against-trough whenever a burst lands in the window. If that is right, the rule should fire at a similar rate on windows where **no fault occurred at all**.


## Method

SK-H1's statistic is imported from `analysis_live_soak_v8`, not re-implemented: median of the 600 s baseline against the maximum of the 300 s recovery window, against the frozen 20% band. It is applied at every minute whose windows clear every injected fault by 900 s.


| | |
|---|---:|
| fault-free windows tested | 1049 |
| of those, deviation > 20% | **411** |
| **false-positive rate** | **39.2%** |
| injected faults scored by V8 | 8 |
| of those, FAILED | 1 (12.5%) |

## Where the one real failure sits in that distribution

V8's failing injection deviated **+75.4%**. Among the 1049 fault-free windows, **94.8%** deviate less than that — so the failure sits at about the **95th percentile of minutes in which no fault was injected at all**. It is not an outlier against the run's own background; it is an ordinary excursion of this metric.


## Reading

**The rule is not discriminative on this metric.** On windows where nothing was injected it exceeds its own band **39.2%** of the time, which over 8 injections predicts **3.1** failures from the oscillation alone. V8 observed **1**. A rule that fires this readily on quiet minutes cannot distinguish a controller that recovered badly from one that was sampled at the wrong phase, so SK-H1's single FAIL is evidence about the rule and the metric — not about recovery.

**What this does not do.** It does not re-score SK-H1, relax its band, or touch `RESULTS_LIVE_SOAK_V8.md`. Changing a frozen rule after seeing its result is what these pre-registrations exist to prevent; measuring what the rule can and cannot resolve is a different act, and it belongs in the limitations rather than in the verdict. The metric itself carries the caveat V5 froze: `crud_p95` is CPU service time, not service latency.
