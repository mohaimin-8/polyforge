## Reading

The column that matters is **GPU corner CORRECTED**. Where it tracks the
v1 column, the correction changed nothing and v1's conclusion transfers.
Where it diverges, the divergence is the finding: v1 priced `large` at
16.6x when the hardware says 1.518x, so any advantage that depended on
an expensive large tier is overstated by v1 and is corrected here.
Aggregate sign flips, if any, are listed verbatim — including them is
the point. All of this holds decisions fixed.
