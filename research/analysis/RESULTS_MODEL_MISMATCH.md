# The MPC with a wrong plant — model-mismatch campaign (V-series)

Generated by `analysis_model_mismatch.py` from `raw_sim_model_mismatch.duckdb`. Pre-registration: `PREREG_MODEL_MISMATCH.md`, committed and pushed before the campaign ran.

**Why this record exists.** `controller.py::_project` calls the same `model.evaluate_step` the engine scores with, so with the realism flags off the controller's model is *bit-identical to the plant*. `model.py:144-147` says as much: an override "moves the world and every controller's beliefs together". Until this campaign the repository contained no experiment in which the controller was wrong about anything, which makes "it was tuned on its own simulator" the sharpest available attack on every result in `RESULTS_MASTER.md`.

`belief_scale` breaks the coupling **inside the projection only**. The plant is untouched, so a plan made under a wrong belief is still scored by the true world — which is the entire experiment.

## Verdict

- **MM-H1** (cost win survives ±25% on every constant): **PASS**.
- **MM-H2** (severity non-inferiority survives ±25% on every constant): **PASS**.

**Scope, stated so it is not over-read.** This campaign perturbs one constant at a time over a ±25% band (with 0.5× and 2.0× on the ladder for shape). It does *not* test compound mismatch, structural mismatch — a plant of a different functional form — or a belief that drifts during a run. Those are separate experiments and are not claimed here.
