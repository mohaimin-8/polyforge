## Notes

* Histogram p95/p99 are route-level (the metric has no tenant label) and are never compared to the replay clock.
* Missing bucket costs (a run whose fine export lacks `cost_usd`) drop that rep from the pairing; `pairs` says how many buckets each CI rests on.
* **Window (Amendments 2 and 4).** The fine export spans the WL-H2 preflight and the teardown seconds around the 300-s window; the pairing uses the span from the first to the last bucket carrying at least half the run's median per-bucket event count, troughs included (`window buckets` above; 30 is the design, 31-32 where the window's edges straddle the 10-s grid), paired by position from the window's first bucket and truncated to the shorter window of a pair. Amendment 2's longest-contiguous-run rule fragmented the window in cells whose demand dips by design; its result is kept in the sensitivity table.
* **Preflight spend is inside `total_cost_usd`.** The WL-H2 knob-liveness gate sends its own requests through the gateway before the window (eleven of them pinned to `large` in the first run, about $0.128 of every run's tier spend), and the run-level export prices them. The gate is identical for every arm, so the offset is common to all run-level costs and absent from the per-bucket pairing.
