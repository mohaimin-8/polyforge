# J comparisons under other lateness weights and without the cap -- EXPLORATORY

Generated by `sensitivity_fair.py`. **Exploratory: nothing here is pre-registered and no registered verdict changes.** The registered J weighs lateness 2 and caps each family's SLO overshoot at 1.0, so a shed AI service (a 30 s outage) scores the same as a 2x miss. This re-scores the confirmatory comparisons of `RESULTS_FAIR_J.md` and `RESULTS_RETUNED.md` with the lateness weight at 1, 2, 4, 8, capped as registered and uncapped (`mean_excess`, the SLO guard's metric). Cost weight 1 and fairness weight 0.5 are held at their registered values. Treatment `jcac_converged`; unit, listwise rule and bootstrap are the frozen scorers' (design points: FAIR_J 60, RETUNED 60; 10,000 resamples, seed 20260926). Negative ΔJ = the treatment is better. The capped w=2 cells reproduce the registered rows exactly.
