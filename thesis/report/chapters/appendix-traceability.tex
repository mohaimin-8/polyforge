\paragraph{Reading order for an examiner.} (1)~\evfile{RESULTS\_MASTER.md}
--- scoreboard, ledger, disambiguation, and the adjudications of sessions
37--38; (2)~\evfile{RECORDS\_INDEX.md} --- every record, generated, with the
hypotheses it names and whether the gate rebuilds it; (3)~the
pre-registrations, in commit order, against their result files
(\evfile{scripts/check\_preregs.py} proves none was edited after its
result); (4)~\evfile{docs/DEFENSE\_QA.md} --- the hard questions
pre-answered; (5)~\evfile{docs/SESSION\_LOG.md} --- the session-by-session
verification record. One command, \evfile{python scripts/reproduce.py},
re-derives every gated record byte-for-byte and every figure and caption
from the committed exports. Without cloning, every pre-registration, record
and figure --- each marked with whether the gate rebuilds it --- is published
at \url{https://mohaimin-8.github.io/polyforge/}, rebuilt from the
repository on every push; the raw run databases are the Zenodo deposit
cited above.
